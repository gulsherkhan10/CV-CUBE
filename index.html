Questions

Quick answers.

How do I get a PDF?

Click Download PDF, then choose “Save as PDF” as the printer. Only the CV is printed.

Is my data safe?

Yes. Everything is saved only in your own browser. Use Reset to clear it.

Will it work with job-application systems?

The templates use plain text and clear headings, which suits most applicant tracking systems. The Classic, Minimal and Compact templates are the safest picks.

Can I change the colours and font?

Yes. Use the accent colour, swatches and font menu above the preview.
